Why one platform

Consolidate the four monitoring silos into one console.

Most plants run separate vendors for effluent, stack, ambient and indoor air: four dashboards, four support contracts, four audit trails. Vepolink collapses them into a single operational picture and a single line of accountability.

01Shared DAS hardware

One multi-protocol acquisition layer serves every monitoring domain on site.

02Serverless cloud

Scales from a single room sensor to a refinery or municipal cluster.

03Unified alerting

One rules engine, per-domain thresholds and one escalation chain.

04Cross-framework reports

CPCB, SPCB and NAAQS workflows from a single trusted dataset.

How it works

From sensor to sign-off,
without switching systems.

Field analyzers and room sensors feed the same acquisition, cloud, dashboard and compliance workflow, so teams can act before a breach becomes a filing problem.

  1. 01Sense

    Water analyzers, stack instruments, ambient monitors and IAQ sensors.

  2. 02Acquire

    Multi-protocol DAS with buffering, tagging and remote configuration.

  3. 03Unify

    Serverless ingest, time-series storage and domain-level normalization.

  4. 04Watch

    Role-specific views for operators, supervisors and compliance teams.

  5. 05Report

    Automated alerts, audit trails and regulator-ready exports.
